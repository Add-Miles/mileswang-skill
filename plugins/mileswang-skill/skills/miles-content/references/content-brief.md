# Content Brief and Deletion Checklist

Use only the fields that affect the deliverable. Do not turn the brief into a questionnaire when the source already answers them.

## Minimum brief

```markdown
Mode: fidelity cleanup | revision | creation
Format and platform:
Audience:
Desired audience action:
Supported one-sentence promise:

Source material:
Verified facts:
Unverified or ambiguous claims:

Scene: who, where, when, action
Want:
Obstacle:
Cost or consequence:
Turn:

Must keep:
Must exclude:
Delivery constraint:
```

## Direction check

- Can the promise be supported by the source?
- Does the piece serve the audience, or mainly display the creator's process and identity?
- Is the angle specific enough to show a scene or decision?
- Would removing the grand conclusion leave a useful piece?
- Is a smaller claim more accurate and more executable?

## Sentence deletion test

For each sentence, ask whether it contributes at least one of these:

- observable scene or action;
- central want, obstacle, cost, or turn;
- necessary evidence or fact boundary;
- decision, consequence, or usable audience payoff;
- transition required for comprehension.

Delete or rewrite the sentence if it contributes none.

## Hard-error gate

Stop delivery until these are resolved or clearly marked:

- fabricated or unsupported fact;
- wrong person, object, source, or chronology;
- missing evidence behind the core claim;
- private or sensitive material outside the agreed scope;
- ambiguity changed into certainty during cleanup;
- promised result not produced by the actual source or workflow.

Treat tone, length, formatting, and local phrasing as soft issues. Repair them without discarding verified substance.
